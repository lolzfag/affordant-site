Open call for data partners

We’re evaluating external datasets and data-generation partners for multimodal models, agent training, and robotics.

We’re particularly interested in proprietary datasets, expert-generated data, teleoperation trajectories, video/action data, and custom collection.

  1. 01Proprietary datasets

    Existing corpora that aren’t available on the open web, with clear provenance and licensing.

  2. 02Expert-generated data

    Annotations, demonstrations and rationales produced by domain specialists rather than crowd workers.

  3. 03Teleoperation trajectories

    Human-operated robot episodes with synchronised observations, actions and task outcomes.

  4. 04Video / action data

    Egocentric or third-person video paired with action labels, for manipulation and agent pretraining.

  5. 05Custom collection

    Partners who can run bespoke collection to a spec: hardware, operators, environments and QA.

Who we are

A small group of researchers from the University of Oxford, the University of Cambridge and Tsinghua University, working on multimodal models, agents and robot learning.

We move quickly on evaluations: a short sample, a data card and a licensing conversation are usually enough to know whether to go further.
